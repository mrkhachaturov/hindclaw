---

Every resource maps onto one object in the management API, so anything you can declare
here you can also read back over REST.

## Identities

`hindclaw_user` and `hindclaw_service_account` create the two kinds of caller. A user is
a person and authenticates with a JWT; a service account authenticates with an API key
and is what an agent should use.

## Grouping

`hindclaw_group` collects users. Policies attach to groups rather than to individuals,
which is what keeps a fleet of agents manageable once it stops being three of them.

## Memory

`hindclaw_bank` creates a memory bank. `hindclaw_policy` decides who may recall from it
and who may retain into it. Both are ordinary resources: a plan shows the change, and
removing a policy from configuration revokes the access on the next apply.
